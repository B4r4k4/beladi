## Known issues (needs review)

- **North Kordofan:** the Commons file `Seal of North Kordofan.png` (also used by Arabic Wikipedia) has the text «ولاية وسط كردفان» (Central Kordofan) written on it, so it is **not** used here. The state flag (a camel) is shown instead until an official North Kordofan emblem is found.
- **Red Sea:** Commons also has a newer CC BY-SA design (`شعار ولاية البحر الأحمر.svg`). The older official public-domain seal is used here.
